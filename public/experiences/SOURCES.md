# Experience imagery

Local illustrative imagery, not verified photographs of bookable venues or operators.

- Wok dish PNGs: https://github.com/Jaykhun/chinese-restaurant-html-template (`img/wok`).
- American-style plate PNGs and food story photographs: https://github.com/codewithsadee/foodie (`assets/images`). Source filenames were visually checked rather than relying on the template's mismatched dish labels.
- Chinese cuisine photograph: https://github.com/mding5692/Beijing-Chinese-Restaurant-Website (`img/food.jpg`), resized for the site.
- Nature/city experience cards also use the existing illustrative images described in `../images/SOURCES.md`.

All activity titles, operators, prices, itineraries and times are fictional demo data. The Sheki cable-car activity is explicitly a concept; this does not assert that a real cable-car operator exists there. Review upstream image rights before commercial publication.
